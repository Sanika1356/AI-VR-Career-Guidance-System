# Pathfinder Limitations and Safeguards

Pathfinder is an educational career-exploration tool. It helps learners notice patterns, compare possibilities, and choose practical learning steps. It does not decide a learner's future and does not replace qualified human advice.

## Assessment and recommendations

Assessment responses and recommendations are directional signals based on the local question bank, submitted profile information, and deterministic scoring rules. They are not psychometric diagnoses, aptitude certifications, admissions decisions, or guarantees of employment, income, or career success.

A ranking is a starting point for reflection. Learners should review several paths, correct information that does not fit, and discuss important decisions with a trusted educator, mentor, or qualified professional.

## AI advisor boundaries

The advisor provides general educational guidance grounded in the career catalog, approved context, and available roadmap information. It can be incomplete, uncertain, or wrong. Its confidence language is not a measure of a learner's ability and is not a professional assessment.

The advisor must not be used for medical, legal, financial, immigration, mental-health, admissions, licensing, or other high-impact decisions. Users should not share passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or sensitive personal information in conversation.

## Resources and sources

Learning resources are project-curated catalog links. A verified label means the link was authored and recorded in the local catalog; it does not guarantee that a third-party page will remain available, current, free, accessible, or suitable for every learner.

Freshness dates and license names describe the catalog record. Users should check the provider's current terms, accessibility statement, cost, prerequisites, and intellectual-property conditions before using a resource.

## VR and WebXR readiness

The career catalog is broader than the current VR catalog. A career can be fully supported without an immersive environment, and the desktop fallback is the primary accessible path when immersive capability is unavailable.

Desktop rendering does not prove headset compatibility. Real WebXR entry, exit, controller behavior, comfort, performance, and touchscreen behavior require the target browser and physical device. Hardware-dependent validation is separate from this page.

## Privacy and control

The system is designed to minimize stored data, keep authentication and server secrets separate, and scope account-owned data to the authenticated user. Optional analytics, personalized AI context, and coarse VR telemetry require explicit consent where those controls are available.

Users can review their profile and use account privacy controls after signing in. Export and deletion flows are account operations; exported data should be protected like any other personal record.
